# Drop-off folder for data you provide

Save downloaded files here and tell Claude their names.

## Wanted

| What | Where to get it (free) | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Solana daily active addresses and transactions, 2021 to today | Artemis (artemis.xyz) or a Dune (dune.com) Solana dashboard, after a free sign-up, using their "download CSV" option | CSV |
| Your Binance.US trading fee rates (maker and taker %) | Binance.US website, Fees page, or `python -m account_tools fees` | Just type the two numbers to Claude |
| Anything from a paid data service you already use (Glassnode, Messari, Token Terminal) | That service's export button | CSV |

## Never put here

- Passwords, API keys, secrets, or recovery phrases
- Screenshots of your account showing personal or balance details

Only public market data, or your fee percentages.
