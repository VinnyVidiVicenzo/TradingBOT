1. IS BITCOIN 'DIGITAL GOLD'?

  Move-together score, Bitcoin vs gold:  +0.06  (144 months)
  Move-together score, Bitcoin vs stocks: +0.35  (144 months)  (for comparison)

  In gold's 12 worst months, Bitcoin fell in 2 of 12 (average +10.7%).
  A real inflation-hedge/safe-haven asset would be expected to move
  independently of, or opposite to, a falling gold price -- not alongside it.

  Verdict: a score of +0.06 is close to zero -- Bitcoin does not
  meaningfully track gold in either direction. The 'digital gold' pitch is
  a marketing comparison of what each asset represents, not a demonstrated
  price relationship in this data.
