Order mattered by more than $20 in 36 of 36 windows.

Why this matters in plain terms: with no withdrawals at all, the order of
the SAME returns would make no difference whatsoever to the ending balance --
multiplying the same numbers in a different sequence gives the same answer.
Every gap shown above exists ONLY because of the $5/month coming out along the
way. A crash early in the withdrawal period locks in a loss on money that a
later recovery can no longer grow, because that money is already gone.

This project's own situation is the opposite -- money going IN, not out -- and
earlier tests already showed that when you are contributing rather than
withdrawing, the timing of a crash matters far less to the end result.
