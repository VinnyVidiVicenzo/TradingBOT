WHY THE LONGER HOLDS ARE NOT A FAIR COMPARISON

Each holding length can only use start dates that leave room to finish,
so the samples cover different eras and overlap heavily. The 5-year rows
all begin inside the 2020-21 run-up; the 3-year rows are full of starts
in the 2022 crash, when everything was cheap. With six years of data
this cannot be fixed, only disclosed -- do not read these as 'three
years is safer than five'.
