WHAT THIS MEANS FOR HOLDING SEVERAL COINS INSTEAD OF ONE

A correlation near +1.00 means a second coin moves in lockstep with Bitcoin --
owning both barely spreads your risk. A correlation well below +1.00 means it
does not, and owning both genuinely diversifies you some of the time.
